Generics are one of TypeScript's most powerful features. They allow you to write functions and types that work with any data type while preserving full compile-time type safety throughout your application pipeline.

## What Are Generics?

Generics let you define placeholder types that get filled in later at the call site. This is essential for creating reusable utilities without casting or using the unsafe any keyword anywhere in your codebase.
